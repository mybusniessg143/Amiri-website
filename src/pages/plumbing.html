What we do not do

We do not carry out gas work of any kind. That includes boilers, central heating boilers, gas hobs, ovens, fires and gas pipework. By law, gas work must be done by a Gas Safe registered engineer.

If you smell gas, leave the property, do not use switches, and call the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999.
