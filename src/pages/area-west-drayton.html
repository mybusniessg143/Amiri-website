Working in West Drayton and Yiewsley

Housing in West Drayton runs from Victorian and Edwardian terraces around the old village and 1930s semis off Station Road to the newer blocks of flats built since the Elizabeth line arrived. Older houses often still have original circuits or an old fuse box that needs replacing with a modern consumer unit, while in the newer flats the calls are more often leaks, failed isolation valves and appliance connections, where a small problem in one flat can quickly reach the one below.

Yiewsley, Harmondsworth, Sipson and the streets around the Grand Union Canal are all close to our base. Because we are local, we can often fit in small jobs and urgent call-outs here that would be harder to reach further away.
