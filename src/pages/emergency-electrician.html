Urgent electrical problems we deal with

If you are not sure whether your problem is urgent, call and describe it. We will tell you honestly.

  • {{icon bolt}}
    Loss of powerThe whole property or part of it has no power, and it is not a wider power cut in your street.
  • {{icon alert}}
    Tripping circuitA breaker or RCD trips and will not stay on, or trips whenever a certain appliance is used.
  • {{icon wrench}}
    Electrical faultSockets, lights or appliances that have suddenly stopped working.
  • {{icon home}}
    Damaged socket or switchCracked, scorched, loose or hot sockets and switches.
  • {{icon bolt}}
    Faulty lightingLights that flicker, buzz or have failed, including emergency lighting in commercial premises.
  • {{icon alert}}
    Burning smell or signs of overheatingA burning or fishy smell, discolouration or heat near sockets, switches or the consumer unit.

While you wait: stay safe

{{icon alert}}

Do not attempt to repair anything yourself.

  • If there is fire, smoke, sparking or someone has had an electric shock, call 999.
  • Do not touch anything that is damaged, hot, wet, sparking or smells of burning.
  • Only make the area safe if you can do so without putting yourself at risk. If it is safe and you know how, switching off at the consumer unit may help; if you are unsure, leave it and keep people away.
  • Stop using any appliance or socket you think is involved.
  • If the power is off in your whole street, check your network operator's power cut service by calling 105.
