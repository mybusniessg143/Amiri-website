Working in Southall

Much of Southall is Victorian and Edwardian terraced housing, much of it extended or converted into flats over the years. Extensions and conversions often leave a patchwork of old and new wiring, so circuits that keep tripping, overloaded consumer units and a lack of sockets in the right places are common reasons people call us. Larger households also put heavy use on kitchens and bathrooms, and that shows up as leaking taps, failed toilet mechanisms and blocked or leaking wastes.

Along The Broadway, King Street and the surrounding parades there are shops, restaurants and small businesses that cannot afford to lose power or water for long. We carry out lighting, socket and fault-finding work for them, as well as plumbing repairs, and plan non-urgent work around opening hours where we can.
