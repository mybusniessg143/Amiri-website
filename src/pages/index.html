02Emergency call-outs

Lost power or got a leak? Call us first.

Emergency call-outs are available for electrical faults and non-gas plumbing problems. Availability changes day to day, so a phone call is the quickest way to find out when we can attend. If we cannot answer, send a WhatsApp message with your postcode and a photo.

{{icon bolt}}Electrical

  • Loss of power to all or part of the property
  • A circuit that keeps tripping
  • Damaged, scorched or hot sockets
  • A burning smell from a fitting

{{icon drop}}Plumbing (non-gas)

  • Leaking or burst accessible pipework
  • An overflowing toilet or cistern
  • A failed isolation valve
  • Leaking wastes under sinks and appliances
