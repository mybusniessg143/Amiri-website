2. The information we collect

We only collect what we need to deal with your enquiry and carry out work. This may include:

  • Contact details: your name, phone number, email address and WhatsApp name or number.
  • Property details: the address and postcode where work is needed, access arrangements, and details of tenants or site contacts where relevant.
  • Job details: a description of the problem, photos or videos you send us, our notes, photos we take of the work, quotes and invoices.
  • Payment and business records: invoices, payments received and related correspondence. We do not store card details on this website.
  • Messages: emails, WhatsApp messages and records of calls (for example, the number and time), and anything you tell us in them.
  • Website data: basic technical information such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, which our hosting and security provider processes to deliver and protect the website.

Please do not send us more personal information than needed, and avoid sending sensitive information (for example about health) unless it is relevant to the job, such as a vulnerable occupant who needs priority.

3. How we receive your information

  • Phone calls to {{phone.display}}.
  • WhatsApp messages, photos and videos.
  • Email to {{email.public}}.
  • The enquiry form on this website. The form does not send or store anything on our website. When you press send, it opens WhatsApp or your own email app with your message pre-filled. Nothing reaches us until you choose to send it from that app.
  • From third parties, for example a letting agent or landlord who gives us a tenant's contact details so we can arrange access for a repair.

6. Website enquiry assistant

Our website has an automated enquiry assistant. It asks a fixed set of questions (the service you need, how urgent it is, your postcode, a description, photos or video, your name, phone number and availability). It does not use artificial intelligence to make decisions about you, and it cannot give prices or confirm bookings. A person reads every enquiry.

  • What happens when you press Submit: your answers, photos and video are sent securely to our website hosting provider, Cloudflare, where they are stored in a private storage area. A job summary is emailed to us through our email delivery provider, Resend, with your photos attached and secure links to your files. We may also receive a short WhatsApp alert that a new enquiry has arrived (urgency, service, postcode and reference only).
  • Why: to reply to your enquiry and, if you ask us to, to quote for and carry out the work (steps at your request before a contract, and our legitimate interests in running the business). We ask you to agree before you submit.
  • How long: enquiries and their photos and video are deleted after 12 months if they do not lead to work. If they do, they become part of the job record (see section 8).
  • Before you submit: the details stay in your browser only. If you leave the page, your answers are kept in your browser for that visit so you can carry on.
  • For security and to prevent misuse, we keep a scrambled (hashed) version of your internet address for a short time to limit repeated submissions.

9. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information, where we no longer have a reason to keep it;
  • ask us to restrict how we use your information, or object to us using it on the basis of legitimate interests;
  • ask for your information in a portable format in some cases;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on consent (for example to use photos).

To make a request, email {{email.public}}. We will respond within one month. There is normally no charge.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, telephone 0303 123 1113.

10. Cookies and website statistics

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and does not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or similar tracking tools. If we ever add optional cookies or tracking, we will ask for your consent first and will not load them until you agree.
