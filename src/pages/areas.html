Our core areas

{{icon pin}}West Drayton and Yiewsley

West Drayton is our home base. Housing here ranges from older terraces and 1930s semi-detached homes to newer flats near the station, and each brings its own jobs: older wiring and consumer units in period properties, and leaks, valves and appliance connections in modern flats where a problem in one unit can quickly affect the one below.

Because we are local, West Drayton, Yiewsley and the surrounding streets are the easiest place for us to attend at short notice.

{{icon pin}}Hayes and Harlington

Hayes has a large number of family homes, rented properties and flats, alongside shops and business units. We help homeowners with everyday electrical and plumbing faults, and landlords and letting agents with tenant-reported repairs and maintenance between tenancies.

For shops and small businesses in and around Hayes town centre we carry out lighting, socket and fault-finding work, planned where possible to avoid disrupting trade.

{{icon pin}}Uxbridge

Uxbridge combines a busy town centre of shops and offices with residential streets and a large rental market, including shared and student housing. That means plenty of call-outs for tripping circuits, overloaded sockets, failed lighting, leaking wastes and worn-out taps and toilet mechanisms.

Landlords and agents in Uxbridge can use us as a contractor who keeps tenants informed and sends photos once a job is done.

{{icon pin}}Hillingdon

Much of Hillingdon is established residential housing: semis, terraces and bungalows, many of which have had extensions and alterations over the years. Typical jobs include adding sockets and lighting, replacing old switches and fittings, finding faults on circuits that have been altered, and fixing leaks and toilet problems.

{{icon pin}}Heathrow and surrounding villages

Around Heathrow, including Harmondsworth, Sipson, Harlington and Longford, there is a mix of homes, rented accommodation and businesses that serve the airport. We help property owners and small businesses with electrical, plumbing and maintenance work, Many people here work shifts, so tell us what times suit you when you get in touch.
