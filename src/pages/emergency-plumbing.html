While you wait: limit the damage safely

{{icon alert}}
  • Turn off the water at the stop tap (stopcock) if you can find it and reach it safely. If there is an isolation valve on the pipe to the leaking fitting, turning that off may be enough.
  • Keep away from electrics. If water is near sockets, light fittings or the consumer unit, do not touch them. Only switch off at the consumer unit if you can reach it without touching water or wet surfaces.
  • If water is coming through a ceiling that is bulging, keep people out of the room underneath.
  • Move valuables away and use towels or buckets to contain water where it is safe to do so.
  • In a flat, let your neighbours or building manager know if water may be reaching them.
