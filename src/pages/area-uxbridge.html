Working in Uxbridge

Uxbridge combines a busy town centre of shops, offices and restaurants with residential streets and a large rental market, including shared and student housing around Brunel University. Shared houses put more load on sockets, circuits, showers and toilets than a typical family home, so we see plenty of tripping circuits, overloaded or damaged sockets, failed lighting, leaking wastes and worn-out cistern parts.

Landlords and agents in Uxbridge can use us as one contractor for electrical, plumbing and general repairs, who keeps tenants informed and sends photos once a job is finished. In Cowley and the quieter residential roads, most jobs are everyday faults, upgrades and repairs in family homes.
