About us

A local building services company you can talk to

Amiri Building Services is based in West Drayton and run by Waris. We carry out electrical work, non-gas plumbing repairs and property maintenance for homeowners, landlords, letting agents and businesses across West London.

Who we are

We set up Amiri Building Services as a limited company to give local customers a straightforward way to get electrical, plumbing and maintenance work done properly, by people who answer the phone and explain things clearly.

How we work

  • {{icon check}}Honest about what we do. We focus on electrical work, non-gas plumbing repairs and general maintenance. We do not do gas work, and if a job needs a different specialist we will say so.
  • {{icon check}}Clear before we start. We explain what we have found, what we recommend and how the work will be charged before carrying it out.
  • {{icon check}}Safety comes first. We will not cut corners on electrical or water safety, even when a quicker fix is requested.
  • {{icon check}}Respect for your property. We protect the work area, tidy up afterwards and keep you updated.
  • {{icon check}}Records you can keep. Photos of completed work and clear invoices, which landlords and agents especially value.
