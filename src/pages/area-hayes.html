Working in Hayes and Harlington

Hayes has a large stock of family homes, rented houses and flats, alongside the shops on Station Road and Coldharbour Lane and business units around the canal and the old industrial estates. A lot of our work here is for landlords and letting agents: tenant-reported faults, leaks and repairs between tenancies, with photos sent back once the job is done.

For shops and small businesses we carry out lighting, socket and fault-finding work, and plan it where possible so that it does not disrupt trading. For homeowners, the usual calls are tripping electrics, extra sockets and lighting, dripping taps, running toilets and leaking wastes.
