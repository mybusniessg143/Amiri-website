Signs you need an electrician

Some electrical problems are an inconvenience; others are a warning. Contact an electrician if you notice any of the following:

  • {{icon check}}A circuit breaker or RCD that trips repeatedly
  • {{icon check}}Sockets or switches that are scorched, cracked, loose or warm to the touch
  • {{icon check}}Lights that flicker or dim when appliances switch on
  • {{icon check}}A burning or fishy smell near sockets, switches or the consumer unit
  • {{icon check}}Buzzing or crackling from fittings
  • {{icon check}}Parts of the property with no power
{{icon alert}}

Do not attempt electrical repairs yourself. If you are worried something is unsafe, stop using it and contact a qualified electrician. If there is fire, smoke or someone has had an electric shock, call 999.
