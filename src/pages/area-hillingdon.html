Working in Hillingdon

Much of Hillingdon is established residential housing: 1930s semis, terraces and bungalows, many of which have had extensions, loft conversions and new kitchens added over the decades. That kind of work often leaves circuits that have been altered several times, so typical jobs include tracing faults, adding sockets and lighting, replacing old switches and fittings, and upgrading consumer units.

On the plumbing side we deal with leaking taps and pipework, toilets that keep running, failed isolation valves and appliance connections for new kitchens. Many of our Hillingdon customers are homeowners who want a job done carefully and explained in plain English, and that is how we work.
