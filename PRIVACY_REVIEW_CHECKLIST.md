# Privacy and terms review checklist

The Privacy notice (`src/pages/privacy.html`) and Terms (`src/pages/terms.html`) are **drafts written as a starting point. They are not legal advice.** Check each point against how the business really operates. For certainty, have them reviewed by a solicitor or use the ICO's guidance for small businesses (ico.org.uk/for-organisations/advice-for-small-organisations).

## Registration and identity
- [ ] Check whether the company must pay the **ICO data protection fee** (ico.org.uk/for-organisations/data-protection-fee). If so, pay it and add the registration number to the privacy notice.
- [ ] Confirm the controller details: AMIRI BUILDING SERVICES LTD, company no. 17472711, jurisdiction verified.
- [ ] Decide the privacy contact address (currently info@; could be waris@).

## What you collect and how
- [ ] Is the list in section 2 accurate? Remove anything you don't collect; add anything you do (e.g. vehicle tracking, CCTV on site, call recordings).
- [ ] **Call recording:** do you record calls? If yes, say so and tell callers.
- [ ] **WhatsApp:** personal WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app? Who has access to the phone?
- [ ] Is the business phone shared with anyone else?
- [ ] Tenants' details from landlords/agents: confirm the wording in section 3 and 4.

## Processors and transfers
- [ ] Name your actual providers: email (Google Workspace), phone provider, accounting/invoicing software, job management app, cloud storage, card payment provider.
- [ ] Check each provider's data processing terms (most offer them in account settings).
- [ ] Confirm international transfer wording (section 7).

## Retention
- [ ] Confirm the retention period for enquiries that don't become jobs (draft: 12 months).
- [ ] Confirm job records are kept 6 years after the end of the financial year (company/HMRC requirement). Your accountant can confirm.
- [ ] Decide when WhatsApp chats and phone photos are cleared down.

## Photos
- [ ] Do you ask permission before using job photos on the website or social media? Keep a record (a WhatsApp "yes" is fine).
- [ ] Avoid house numbers, number plates, faces, documents and post in photos.

## Security
- [ ] Two-step verification on Google Workspace, WhatsApp, Cloudflare, GitHub.
- [ ] Passcodes on phones/laptops used for work.
- [ ] Remove access for anyone who leaves.

## Cookies and tracking
- [ ] Confirm no tracking cookies or pixels are on the site (none are included).
- [ ] If you later add Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel, embedded maps/videos or chat widgets from other companies: add a **consent banner that blocks them until the visitor agrees**, update section 10, and update the CSP in `static/_headers`.

## Future AI receptionist
- [ ] Before enabling it: update section 6 with the provider names, what is processed, retention of chat logs, and how to reach a human.
- [ ] Consider a short Data Protection Impact Assessment (ICO template) because it involves new technology processing customer messages.

## Terms
- [ ] Complete or remove every `[OWNER TO CONFIRM]` item in section 5: call-out charges, quotations, parts/materials, payment terms, cancellations, guarantees.
- [ ] Consumer cancellation rights: jobs agreed at the customer's home are usually "off-premises contracts" with a 14-day cancellation period, and customers must be told about it in writing; there are rules for urgent repairs requested by the customer. Get advice and prepare a short written job/quote template that includes the required information.
- [ ] Make sure the terms on the website match what you send with quotes and invoices.
- [ ] Update the "Last updated" dates on both pages.
